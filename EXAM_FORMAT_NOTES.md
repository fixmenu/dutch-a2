# A2 Inburgeringsexamen — format notes (own summary, Oct 2026)

These notes describe the *format* only. No official question, text, audio script, image or answer is stored in this repo. All bank items in `data/bank.js` are newly written.

## General (DUO)
- Each exam part is taken separately. There are 15 minutes of instructions beforehand.
- Lezen, Luisteren, Spreken and KNM are taken on a computer. Schrijven is on paper.
- Durations: Lezen 65 min · Luisteren 45 min · Schrijven 40 min · Spreken 35 min · KNM 45 min.
- DUO publishes no official pass mark (cesuur) for the language parts. The pass marks in the app are estimates.

## Lezen (25 MC questions, 65 min)
- About 9–10 short everyday texts with 2–4 questions each, 25 questions in total.
- Text types: letters (gemeente, school, doctor, landlord), e-mails at work, ads, signs/notices, schedules and timetables, folders, leaflets.
- Mostly 3 answer options (A/B/C), sometimes 4.
- Questions ask for a detail, the purpose of a text, or what you should do.
- Some questions require combining two pieces of information (e.g. time + 5 minutes early, price + surcharge, table lookups).
- Typical distractor: a word that does appear in the text but answers a different question.
- Score guidance in the official oefenexamen: roughly 17–22 correct = reasonable chance, 23–25 = big chance.

## Luisteren (25 MC questions, 45 min)
- Short fragments: dialogues, phone calls and voicemails, announcements (station, shop, school), radio.
- Usually 1 (sometimes 2) questions per fragment, 3 options.
- Fragments can be replayed, but the clock keeps running.
- Traps: times ("kwart over/voor", "half acht"), numbers/prices, "niet … maar …", a changed plan in the dialogue.

## Schrijven (4 tasks, 40 min, pen and paper)
Instruction: answer 4 questions; extra paper is not allowed. Task types seen across the 3 official PDFs:
1. **E-mail.** A situation plus 3–4 bullet points. The From/To/Subject header, the salutation and the closing are pre-printed. "Schrijf in hele zinnen."
2. **Form.** Personal fields plus a tick-box choice plus 1–2 open questions. "Some data you have to invent yourself."
3. **Short text for a (wijk)krant / notice board.** At least three sentences; "Denk aan:" followed by 3 guiding questions.
4. **Note/message from pictures.** Write three things to a colleague, neighbour, etc. The opening and closing are given.

## Spreken (since 1 March 2025: 16 items, 4 parts, 35 min)
- There are no multiple-choice speaking items any more.
- Each answer takes about 1 minute and is recorded.
- Part 1: a person in a video asks a personal question (often with "Vertel ook …").
- Part 2: one picture plus a two-part question (describe + why/when/what do you do).
- Part 3: two pictures; say which you prefer ("liever") and why.
- Part 4: three pictures forming a story; tell what happens in all three (eerst/daarna/ten slotte).

## KNM (new exam from 1 July 2025)
- 40 MC questions, 3 options, 45 minutes. Pass ≈ 28/40 (reported by test-prep sites; not an official DUO figure).
- Knowledge questions, often with a photo. The old intro videos are gone.
- 8 themes: werk en inkomen · omgangsvormen/waarden/normen · wonen · gezondheid en zorg · geschiedenis en geografie · instanties · staatsinrichting en rechtsstaat · opvoeding en onderwijs.
- New emphasis areas: the Holocaust and antisemitism, women's self-determination, law above religion/culture, the role of the POH at the GP, and DigiD/digital government.

## How the app mirrors this
| Part | Bank size | Mock structure |
|---|---|---|
| Lezen | 40 texts / 123 Qs (3–4 opts) | 25 Q, 65 min, texts kept together |
| Luisteren | 40 fragments / 61 Qs (3 opts), TTS with 2 voices | 25 Q, 45 min, replay allowed |
| KNM | 156 Qs, 8 themes, 3 opts | 40 Q (5 per theme), 45 min, pass 28 |
| Schrijven | 20 tasks (7 e-mail, 5 form, 4 wijkkrant, 4 briefje) | 4 tasks: e-mail, form, krant/briefje, briefje/e-mail; 40 min |
| Spreken | 30 tasks (8/8/7/7 per part) | 16 tasks = 4 per part, 35 min |

Schrijven/Spreken scoring in the app is an automatic heuristic (length, sentences, content keywords). It is not the official rubric.
